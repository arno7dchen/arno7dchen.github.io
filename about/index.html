About Sunburst Outdoor | China Metal Frame Patio Outdoor Furniture Manufacturer

Founded in 2017, Sunburst Outdoor Products Co., Ltd. is a professional manufacturer of high-quality metal frame patio outdoor furniture. Our core business covers outdoor furniture, patio accessories (cushions and parasols) and other related outdoor series products.

Adhering to the business philosophy of "Better, all-ways", we always use durable raw materials and implement rigorous quality control in every production procedure. This ensures that every product we deliver meets international standards and customer expectations.

We work with global buyers from sampling to container loading. We offer standard products and customized solutions to meet your diverse needs with fast response and reliable delivery.
